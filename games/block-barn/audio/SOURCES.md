# Block Barn sounds

The default voice is Brian's: his recordings of each letter ("C c"), each animal's name ("cow") and, where he makes one, the animal's noise ("moo") go in this folder as `letter-<x>.m4a`, `name-<x>.m4a` and `noise-<x>.m4a`, and their keys go in `clips.js`. They are his own work and are not released as CC0: each is listed below tagged `studio-owned`. Any other sound file must be CC0 or public domain, verified on its own page, and listed here with its URL.

Until a clip exists the game uses the device's own speech, and its sound effects (block clack, tumble, hay puff) are synthesised in code.
