For grown-ups

Block Barn is made for a little one to play alone: tap a block to hear its letter and meet its animal, tap again to stack it, tap the tower to knock it down. Nothing here can be lost, and nothing leaves this device.

Sound

Favourite letters

Pinned letters come around more often (the letters of your child's name, say).

Keep her in the game

On iPhone or iPad, turn on Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access). Then triple-click the side or Home button while Block Barn is open to lock the device to it. Triple-click again and enter your passcode to leave.

Keep your recordings

On iPhone or iPad, add Block Barn to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), then open it and record from the Home Screen icon. Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate recordings, and Safari may clear a website's saved data after a few weeks without a visit.
